2Car, petrol and electric comparison

The car list holds my estimates of real-world miles per gallon. Overtype the figure with your own.

Road miles come from OpenStreetMap routing. Petrol cost = miles divided by miles per gallon, times 4.546 litres, times the price you choose. Electric cost = miles divided by miles per kWh (for the season), times the tariff rate. Only the position of the start or of where you are, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to find nearby prices.

Price-drop alerts

How alerts work (and what stops them being annoying)

An alert looks like: Price drop at Tesco Andover, SP10 1UZ: was 139.9p, now 136.9p. Ranked 4th (was 7th).

  • Only real drops of at least your chosen size, at a station that is now in your top 10 for your fuel and distance.
  • At most one alert per check, at most 3 an hour and 6 a day.
  • The same station is not reported to you again for 2 hours.
  • None between 10 at night and 7 in the morning.
  • Your phone will ask once: Allow MyICE to send notifications? Choose Allow.
  • The service keeps only the phone's private alert address, the position of your postcode rounded to about 1 km, your fuel and your settings.

About the prices

Petrol and diesel prices come from the government Fuel Finder service (Open Government Licence) and are set by the stations themselves, so they can be a little behind the pump. Report a discrepancy. Electricity rates and the car figures are estimates: check them. MyICE has no adverts and no tracking. Companion to MyEV Calculator (the electric-car version of this app).
